# Reflection Response — Class 1

## Question

> OpenAI was founded in 2015 as a non-profit. By 2023, it was valued at $80 billion. What does that trajectory tell you about the relationship between research ambition and commercial opportunity in AI?

---

## My Response

OpenAI’s journey from a non-profit research lab in 2015 to an $80 billion company by 2023 shows that ambitious research in AI can create enormous commercial value when the technology becomes useful at scale.

This trajectory proves that breakthroughs in AI are not just academic achievements — they can transform industries, businesses, and everyday life. OpenAI initially focused on advancing artificial intelligence safely and openly, but tools like ChatGPT demonstrated how research can evolve into products used by millions of people worldwide.

The growth of OpenAI also highlights the increasing importance of AI in the global economy. Investors and companies are willing to invest billions because AI is becoming a foundational technology, similar to the internet or smartphones.

Overall, this journey shows that strong research ambition in AI can become a catalyst for innovation, global influence, and massive economic opportunity.
