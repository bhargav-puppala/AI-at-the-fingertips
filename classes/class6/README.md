# Reflection Response — Class 6

## Question

> Go to the homepage of any AI product you have heard of — Notion, Linear, Vercel, or any startup. Look only at the hero section. What is the headline? What is the CTA? What emotion does the design create? Do you think it works? Why or why not?

---

## My Response

I looked at the hero section of Notion.

The headline is simple and clear. It focuses on helping people organize their work, notes, and ideas in one place. The CTA is also very direct, usually something like “Get Notion free” or “Start building.” Instead of adding too much text, the hero section quickly explains what the product does and encourages users to try it immediately.

The design creates a calm and productive feeling. It looks clean, modern, and not overwhelming. There is a lot of white space, simple colors, and smooth visuals, which makes the product feel easy to use even for beginners.

I think the hero section works really well because within a few seconds, a visitor can understand what the product is about and what action to take next. It does not try to impress with too many animations or complicated designs. Instead, it focuses on clarity and trust, which is probably why many people like using Notion.
